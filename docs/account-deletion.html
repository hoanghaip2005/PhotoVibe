VibeLens Account Deletion

Last updated: May 19, 2026

This page explains how VibeLens users can request deletion of their account and associated server-side data.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send a deletion request to the developer contact email listed on the VibeLens Google Play Store listing.
  2. Use the subject line: VibeLens account deletion.
  3. Include the email address associated with your VibeLens account.
  4. We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account before deleting server-side data.

What Data Is Deleted

After verification, we will delete or anonymize account-related server-side data where reasonably possible, including profile data, saved vibe results, music preferences, saved playlists, and saved filters.

What May Be Retained

Some records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, payment records, backups, or dispute resolution. Google Play purchase records are managed by Google.

Local Data

Local-only data stored on your device can be removed by deleting the app or clearing app storage in your device settings.

Privacy details are available in the VibeLens Privacy Policy.
