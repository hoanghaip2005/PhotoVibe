VibeLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

VibeLens turns a photo into an AI-generated vibe result, including a playlist, quote, filter suggestion, and story card. This policy explains what information VibeLens collects, how it is used, and how users can request deletion.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, user ID, and optional display name when you create or sign in to an account.
  • Photos: images you choose from your gallery or capture in the app for AI vibe analysis.
  • App activity: vibe results, saved results, saved playlists, saved filters, music preferences, and related app settings.
  • Purchase information: purchase status and entitlement information for subscriptions or credit packs purchased through Google Play Billing.

How We Use Information

  • To sign users in and manage account sessions.
  • To analyze selected photos and generate vibe results.
  • To personalize playlist and filter suggestions.
  • To save user-selected results and preferences.
  • To process subscriptions, credit packs, and entitlements.
  • To protect the app from abuse and troubleshoot service issues.

Photos and AI Processing

When you choose to analyze a photo, VibeLens sends the image to the VibeLens backend and configured AI service providers so the app can generate a result. Do not upload photos that you do not have permission to use.

Third-Party Service Providers

VibeLens uses service providers to operate the app, including:

  • Supabase for authentication and database services.
  • AI service providers for image analysis and text generation.
  • Google Play Billing for subscriptions and in-app purchases.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate VibeLens, comply with law, process purchases, protect users, or maintain app security.

Data Security

VibeLens uses encryption in transit for network requests. No system is perfectly secure, but we use reasonable safeguards to protect account and app data.

Data Retention

Local-only data remains on your device unless you delete the app or clear app data. Account data may be kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period after deletion requests when required for security, billing, legal, or backup purposes.

Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your VibeLens account and associated server-side data by following the instructions on the VibeLens account deletion page. Local-only data can be removed by deleting the app or clearing app storage on your device.

Children

VibeLens is not designed for children. The intended audience is adults. If you believe a child has provided personal information, request deletion using the account deletion page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The updated date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, use the developer contact email listed on the VibeLens Google Play Store listing and include the email address associated with your VibeLens account.
